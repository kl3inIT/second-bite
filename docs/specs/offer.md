# offer

Owns the surplus sellers post and the settings every offer follows (tables `offer_*`). Decided in the [SEC-11 design](../increments/active/sec-11-offer-module/design.md#the-flow). The sources: Open Food Network for units and prices kept on the offer and copied by an order; Too Good To Go for the price against an original price, the kinds and a sold-out offer kept visible; Umi Feeds for a collection window with a start and an end; foodsharing for reading expiry from the window in every query, so nothing waits for a job; Karrot for refusing to move what is booked. Checks: [docs/tests/offer.md](../tests/offer.md).

An offer carries its status, units left, discount, photo addresses (`photo` is the cover; `photos` is every photo, the cover first) and the collection point copied from the seller when it was posted. Prices are whole đồng; times are RFC 3339.

Published to other modules: `Service` and `WindowChangedEvent`. `order` reads the windows of the offers it holds with `WindowsOf`, and takes units with `Take` and gives them back with `Return`, both inside the order's transaction; `Take` answers what the order copies (`Taken`: the seller, its name and collection point, the offer's name, unit, prices, photo and window).

## Rules

- **Fields**: a name of 1 to 80 characters; a kind among `bakery`, `prepared_food`, `ingredients`, `drinks`, `mixed_box`, `other`; a unit of up to 20 characters, `suất` when empty; 1 to 999 units; a price of at least 1,000 đồng below the original price; an optional weight per unit, 1 g to 100 kg; a description of up to 1,000 characters, required for a `mixed_box`; a photo, and up to two more after it (SEC-119).
- **Settings** (admins change them while the service runs; the first values are the team's of 18 October 2026): the price at least 20% below the original price; a window of 30 minutes to 6 hours, not over, starting within 24 hours; a search radius of 5 km by default and 30 at most, read by search (step 4). A setting applies to what is posted, and to a price or window changed, after it is saved.
- **The photos** are a cover (`photoId`) and up to two more (`morePhotoIds`, replaced as a whole by a change), each one the poster uploaded, which the offer claims, or one an earlier offer or dish of the same seller shows: that is how staff post again, sending an earlier offer's details with new units and window. Another seller's photo answers `FILE_PHOTO_NOT_FOUND`; a repeated photo, the cover again among the others, or more than two others answers `OFFER_DETAILS_INVALID`. Lists, search and orders show the cover; an order copies the cover only.
- **Status**: `on_sale`, `withdrawn` and `closed` are stored; `sold_out` (no unit left) is read in every query, and so is `closed` (the window is over), so an offer closes the moment its window ends. A job every minute (`offer.close_ended`) stores `closed` on the offers still `on_sale` whose window is over, so the table follows (SEC-96). A sold-out offer stays visible; a closed or withdrawn one is seen only by its seller's people and admins.
- **Changes**: before any unit is ordered, everything but the seller and the collection point; once units are ordered, only the description, the photo, the units (never below the units ordered) and the collection window, since an order keeps the price and the name it was placed at and follows the window. A change of the window of an offer with orders publishes `offer.window_changed`, and `order` moves the orders still to collect to it (SEC-91); the customers are not told, as they leave no address to tell them at. Nothing changes once the offer is closed or withdrawn. Changes to one offer are serialized by a row lock.
- **Withdrawing** keeps the row with when it was withdrawn; a closed offer cannot be withdrawn. Withdrawing publishes `offer.withdrawn` in the same transaction, and `order` refuses the orders still to collect on the offer ([SEC-108](../increments/active/sec-108-withdraw-with-orders/design.md)).
- **Taking units** for an order is one conditional update: only an offer on sale, its window not over, with the units left; when two orders want the last unit, one gets it. Units come back when an order is cancelled, never above the units posted.
- Admins neither post nor change offers; a stranger gets 404.
- **The catalog** ([SEC-55 design](../increments/completed/sec-55-seller-catalog/design.md)) holds the stable base of a dish: a photo, a name of 1 to 80 characters, a kind, a unit (`suất` when empty), a description, the ingredients and allergens (up to 500 characters), an original price (optional, from 1,001 đồng; `0` in a change takes it away), and whether it is hidden from the list a post is picked from. It has no selling price, hours or units; its original price is only where a post starts from. It is private to the seller's people; an admin or a stranger gets 404. At most 100 dishes per seller, the limit holding under concurrency (an advisory lock). A post copies the dish, so changing or deleting a dish never touches an offer; a dish's photos count as ones the seller shows, so a post may use them.
- **Search** keeps offers on sale and sold out, the window not over, the seller not blocked (the blocked sellers are left out in the query, so a page is never short), then filters: within a radius of a point (the settings' default, at most their maximum), words, kinds, a maximum price, a collection-time range the window overlaps, one seller, and, when asked, nothing sold out. Its orders: `relevance` (default with words), `distance` (default with a point), `ending_soon` (default otherwise), `price`, `discount`.
- **Words** match the name, the seller's name and the description, weighted in that order, every word required and the last one also as a prefix. Each syllable is indexed under a loose form without marks and an exact form with its marks and its tone as a digit, so a word typed without marks matches any marks ("ca": "cá", "cà") and one typed with marks only those ("cá"); the tone's place ("hòa", "hoà"), the encoding (NFC or decomposed), zero-width characters and a lone "y" after h, k, l, m, s or t ("mỳ", "mì") make no difference. The service writes the `tsvector` itself (`internal/offer/search_text.go`); words side by side rank first ([why](../increments/active/sec-11-offer-module/design.md#vietnamese-words)).

## Data

| Table            | Holds                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Lifecycle                                               |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| `offer_offers`   | Seller and who posted (no foreign key across modules), state, the fields, units and units left (`0 <= units_left <= units`), the cover photo and up to two more (`more_photo_ids`), window, the copied latitude, longitude, generated location (GiST index), address and seller's name, the words searched (`search_vector`, GIN index), when posted, changed and withdrawn | Kept: orders, measures and the seller's history read it |
| `offer_dishes`   | Seller, who added it (no foreign keys across modules), the dish's fields, hidden or not, the cover photo and up to two more (`more_photo_ids`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Kept until the seller deletes it                        |
| `offer_settings` | One row: the settings, who changed them and when                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Replaced on save                                        |

## Failures

| Code                       | Status | When                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------- | ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `OFFER_DETAILS_INVALID`    | 400    | A field breaks its rule                                                                                                           |
| `OFFER_DISCOUNT_TOO_SMALL` | 400    | The price is not the minimum discount below the original price                                                                    |
| `OFFER_WINDOW_INVALID`     | 400    | The window is too short, too long, over, or starts too late                                                                       |
| `OFFER_SEARCH_INVALID`     | 400    | A point off the map, a time range ending before it starts, a lowest price above the highest, or sorting by distance from no point |
| `OFFER_SETTINGS_INVALID`   | 400    | Settings out of range or contradicting each other                                                                                 |
| `OFFER_DISH_LIMIT_REACHED` | 409    | A seller already holds 100 dishes                                                                                                 |
| `OFFER_DISH_NOT_FOUND`     | 404    | No such dish, or one of another seller                                                                                            |
| `OFFER_NOT_FOUND`          | 404    | No such offer, or one the caller may not see or change                                                                            |
| `OFFER_CHANGE_NOT_ALLOWED` | 409    | A change its orders forbid, or any change once it is closed or withdrawn                                                          |
| `OFFER_SOLD_OUT`           | 409    | `Take`: fewer units left than an order asks; `order` answers it as its own code                                                   |
| `OFFER_UNAVAILABLE`        | 409    | `Take`: the offer is withdrawn or closed; `order` answers it as its own code                                                      |
| `SELLER_NOT_ACTIVE`        | 403    | Posting or changing for a seller that is not active                                                                               |
| `SELLER_NOT_FOUND`         | 404    | Posting for a seller the caller is not a member of                                                                                |
| `FILE_PHOTO_NOT_FOUND`     | 404    | A photo neither the caller's nor the seller's                                                                                     |
