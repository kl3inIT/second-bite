# stats

Owns the pages people open on the site, counted for the prototype's numbers (SEC-80, [design](../increments/completed/sec-80-prototype-numbers/design.md)): one row per view, and the salt of each day (tables `stats_views` and `stats_salts`). No analytics service is used.

## Operations

| Operation                            | Who                               | Answers                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `POST /stats/views` `{path, source}` | Anyone, with or without a session | `204`, whether the view is kept or dropped as a bot's or a prefetch's. A body out of its contract, such as an empty path, answers `PLATFORM_REQUEST_INVALID` (400); the web ignores the answer either way. |
| `GET /admin/reports?since=&until=&sellerId=&timeFrom=&timeUntil=` | Admin | The report for days in Vietnam, both included: the last 7 up to today by default, at most 92; with `timeFrom` and `timeUntil` (HH:MM, `24:00` ending at midnight), only the posts and orders whose window starts, and the views opened, in that part of each day, new users and customers staying the whole day's. `totals`; `days`, every day of the range; `stores`, each store with numbers, the most orders first; `storeDays`, each store on each day it has numbers. Each holds, under `counts`, `views`, `people`, `homeViews`, `qrViews`, `offers`, `unitsPosted`, `held`, `unitsHeld`, `collected`, `unitsCollected`, `collectedByCustomer` (the collected orders their customer confirmed, "Đã nhận món", the store not having pressed "Đã giao" first), `cancelled`, `refused`, `expired`, `collectedVnd`, `newAccounts` and `newCustomers`, and for the orders collected `originalVnd` (their dishes at the original price), `discountVnd` and `discountOrders` (the discounts Second Bite paid back) and `gramsCollected` with `unitsWeighed` (the grams of the units whose dish gives a weight, and how many those are); each store also `daysPosted` and `fullDays`; `accounts`, the accounts at the range's end; and `customers` and `returningCustomers` (SEC-200). With `sellerId`, only that store, and its views are its page's and its dishes'; new users stay the site's. A range reversed or too long, or a part of the day with one end or not starting before it ends, answers `REPORT_RANGE_INVALID` (400). |
| `GET /admin/reports/export?since=&until=&sellerId=&timeFrom=&timeUntil=` | Admin | The same report as a CSV file in Vietnamese, `text/csv; charset=utf-8` with a byte order mark, named `bao-cao-<since>-den-<until>.csv`: a header, then for each day a row for the whole site (`Cả trang`, without `sellerId`) and a row per store with numbers, `Tổng`, then a `Tổng` row per store with its days posted and full days (SEC-200); the share collected in whole percent, empty without orders. Each row ends with "Giá gốc phần đã lấy", "Khách tiết kiệm" (the original price less what customers paid: the total less the discount), "Bù giảm giá" and "Thực phẩm được cứu (gam)". A store's name that starts with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, a tab or a carriage return is written behind an apostrophe. |
| `GET /admin/reports/analysis?since=&until=&sellerId=&timeFrom=&timeUntil=` | Admin | For the report's days and store, posts and orders counted on their collection day: the portions posted and collected by kind and by discount band (`free`, `from70`, `from50`, `from30`, `under30`); the five dishes most collected and the five ended ones with the most portions not collected; how long posts took to sell out (four bands, those ended without, the median in minutes); the orders by weekday and hour of the window's start; how long before the window they were placed; how those not collected ended (`cancelled`, `expired`, `refused_` and the reason); the customers by orders handed over (1, 2, 3 for more); the reviews' tags; the problems reported; and the views by source, the site's whatever store is chosen. A range reversed or too long answers `REPORT_RANGE_INVALID`. |
| `GET /admin/reports/workbook?since=&until=&sellerId=&timeFrom=&timeUntil=` | Admin | The report and its analysis as an Excel workbook, its summary naming the part of the day kept, `application/vnd.openxmlformats-officedocument.spreadsheetml.sheet`, named `bao-cao-<since>-den-<until>.xlsx`: the sheets "Tổng quan", "Theo ngày và cửa hàng" (the CSV's rows), "Theo loại món", "Mức giảm giá", "Món", "Đơn không thành" and "Thứ và giờ", in Vietnamese; figures are numbers and names text, never formulas (github.com/xuri/excelize/v2). |
| `GET /admin/shift-reports?since=&until=&sellerId=&shift=…` | Admin | The orders and their buyers by day and shift (SEC-209): `since` and `until` days in Vietnam, both required, at most 31 days, none after today; each `shift` is `HH:MM-HH:MM,Name` (the parameter repeats, one to six, in order, not overlapping, a name of 1 to 40 characters), the defaults Ca sáng 06:00–12:00, Ca chiều 12:00–18:00 and Ca tối 18:00–24:00 when none. `days`, every day of the range, each with `summary` (the whole day, whatever the shifts) and `shifts`, each with `name`, `from`, `until`, a `summary` and its `orders`, by window then number: number, store, customer and phone (the contact copied on the order, empty once forgotten), dishes, units, `orderedAt`, `windowStart`, `windowEnd` and `status`; `totals` for the range. A summary holds `stores` (with a post or an order), `unitsPosted`, `orders`, `unitsCollected` and `unitsNotCollected` (refused or expired; cancelled and open orders are in neither). `REPORT_SHIFT_RANGE_INVALID` (400) for a range reversed, ending after today or longer than 31 days; `REPORT_SHIFTS_INVALID` (400) for shifts that break the rules. |
| `GET /admin/shift-reports/export?` (the same query) | Admin | The same answer as an Excel workbook, `application/vnd.openxmlformats-officedocument.spreadsheetml.sheet`, named `bao-cao-theo-ca-<since>-den-<until>.xlsx`: the sheet "Tổng hợp" (a row per day and shift, the day, and the totals) and a sheet "Ngày dd-mm" per day with its summary and a table per shift of its orders and buyers; figures are numbers, names, phones and times text, never formulas. |

Published to other modules: nothing. It asks `identity.AccountDays`, `offer.PostDays`, `SellersOf`, `WeightsOf` and `PostsBetween`, `order.OrderDays`, `ListForAdmins`, `FirstOrderDays`, `CollectedCustomers`, `OfferOutcomes` and `Analysis`, and `seller.NamesOf`.

## Rules

- A view is dropped, not refused, when its User-Agent is a bot's or missing, when it comes from a data centre's addresses, or when the browser only fetched the page ahead (`Purpose`, `Sec-Purpose`), as `zgo.at/isbot` reads them; and past 600 views from one address in ten minutes (SEC-160).
- The page is read from the path, with or without the locale: `/` is the home, `/sellers/{id}` a store and `/offers/{id}` a dish, with the id kept; anything else is `other`. A query string is ignored.
- The source is `utm_source` as the web read it, lowercased: 1 to 40 of `a-z`, `0-9`, `_` and `-`, else none.
- The day is the calendar day in Vietnam (UTC+7) when the view arrives.
- The visitor is `sha256(day's salt ‖ address ‖ User-Agent)`, so one browser counts once a day and cannot be followed from one day to the next. The salt is random, stored for its day and deleted after it. No cookie, address or User-Agent is stored.
- The address is the first of `X-Forwarded-For`, as the site is reached only through the proxy, else the connection's.
- Posts and orders count on their collection day, the day their window starts in Vietnam, as the admins' orders list goes by it. Every offer counts, whatever became of it. An order counts as held whatever became of it, and as collected, cancelled, refused or expired by how it ended; one confirmed or ready whose window is over is expired. A collected order counts the same whether staff handed it over or its customer confirmed it; `collectedByCustomer` tells how many its customers confirmed. The CSV and the workbook leave that count out: the CSV's columns are pasted into the sponsor's sheet.
- A store's views are those of its page and of its dishes' pages; a browser that opened both on a day is one person. People add up over days, since a visitor's key changes every day.
- The web sends a view once a page is on screen, never for a link the router fetched ahead, and not for the admin's or a store's own pages, nor from a browser a program drives (`navigator.webdriver`); a view that fails is lost.
- **New users** (SEC-192) are the site's, whatever store is chosen, and none of a store's rows has them: `newAccounts`, the accounts created on the day in Vietnam, and `newCustomers`, the customers whose first order, told apart by phone number, is collected on the day. An order whose contact was forgotten counts for nobody, and a deleted account on no day. `accounts` is the accounts there are at the end of the range. The CSV writes them on the whole site's rows and the totals, as "Tài khoản mới" and "Khách đặt lần đầu".
- **The support programme's figures** (SEC-200, [design](../increments/active/buyer-discount/design.md#the-programmes-figures-step-4-sec-200)): a store's `daysPosted` are the days of the range it has a post on, whatever became of it, and its `fullDays` those it posted at least 7 portions on (`fullDayUnits`). `customers` are the phone numbers with an order handed over whose collection day is in the range, and `returningCustomers` those of them with orders handed over on at least two collection days up to the range's end, earlier days included. Both are the site's whatever store is chosen; the screen shows their share as "Khách mua lại", and the CSV writes them on the site's `Tổng` as "Khách nhận đơn" and "Khách mua lại".
- The nightly job `stats.forget_old` deletes the salts of the days that are over and the views older than 400 days. Running it again changes nothing more.

## Data

| Table         | Holds                                                                                            | Lifecycle                    |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| `stats_views` | The day, when, the kind of page, the store or dish (no foreign key), the source, the visitor key | Deleted after 400 days       |
| `stats_salts` | The salt of a day                                                                                | Deleted once its day is over |
