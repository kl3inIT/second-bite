# order

Owns what customers order: units of one seller's offers, collected at the counter within one window, by a named customer with a phone number, signed in or not (tables `order_*`). Decided in the [SEC-15 design](../increments/completed/sec-15-order-module/design.md); ordering without an account in [SEC-76 decision 7](../increments/active/sec-76-web-customer-flow/design.md); collecting without a code in [SEC-86](../increments/active/sec-86-collect-without-code/design.md); the customer's review in SEC-97. The sources: the pilot domain model for confirming an order when it is placed, as Too Good To Go, Karrot and Open Food Network do; Open Food Network for copying the name and price onto the order line and logging every transition; foodpanda and Keeta for one cart per seller; offer's own rule of reading expiry from the window, so nothing waits for a job. Checks: [docs/tests/order.md](../tests/order.md).

During the pilot the customer pays the seller at the counter; the module records no payment.

An order carries its number (`SB-` and a number), status, seller (name, address, collection point), lines (offer, name, unit, price, original price, units, photo), total, window, the time of each transition, the cancel or refusal reason, its checkout, who collects (name and phone number), its review once rated (stars, tags, comment, time) and, to its customer, the access.

Published to other modules: `Service` alone.

It follows `seller.LocationChangedEvent` (listener `order.follow_seller_location`): the orders still to collect (`confirmed`, `ready`) copy the seller's collection point as it stands and tell the sockets following them, so a customer on the way reads where the seller now is; orders handed over, cancelled, refused or expired keep theirs, and the same event twice changes nothing more (SEC-66). The customers are not told otherwise.

## Rules

- **A cart**: one to ten different offers of one active seller, each one or more units. The units are taken from each offer in the order's transaction, in one order of identifiers; when one offer has too few, nothing is taken.
- **The window** is the part all the offers' windows share; it must still be to come. When a seller moves the window of an offer that has orders, `offer.window_changed` reaches `order`, which reads the windows of each open order's offers again and moves the order to the part they share (SEC-91); an order whose offers no longer share a time keeps its window, and orders handed over, cancelled or refused stay as they were. Each order moved is moved in its own transaction, and its customer is told the new hours by push; a window already in place changes nothing and tells nobody.
- **Confirmed at once**; staff never accept an order.
- **The checkout**: the orders placed together from one cart, one per seller, share an identifier the client gives, or a new one; it groups them for their customer and changes nothing else.
- **Who collects**: a name of 1 to 50 characters and a Vietnamese phone number, `0` and nine digits; spaces, dots, dashes and `+84` are taken out or written as `0`. Never logged. `byZalo` records when the customer agreed at checkout to be told by Zalo (`zalo_consent_at`); nothing sends there yet (SEC-25).
- **Idempotency**: the same `Idempotency-Key` from the same account, or without an account with the same phone number, returns the order placed first, also when both arrive at once. The key is 16 to 255 characters, so it cannot be guessed to read an order back with its access (SEC-167); the web sends a random UUID.
- **The access**: 122 random bits stored with the order, compared in constant time. It proves the order is theirs to whoever placed it without signing in; it is sent as a header, never in an address.
- **Status**: `confirmed`, `ready`, `collected`, `cancelled`, `refused` and `expired` are stored; `expired` is also read in every query when an order still confirmed or ready has its window over, so it expires the moment its window ends. A job every minute (`order.expire_ended`) stores `expired`, with an `expired` event, on the orders still to collect whose window is over, so the table follows (SEC-96); the customer is not told. Expired units are not offered again.
- **Cancelling**: by the customer, signed in or with the access, while the order is confirmed: until the seller marks it ready, even once the window has opened (SEC-121); the units return to their offers. A ready, ended or expired order answers `ORDER_CANCEL_TOO_LATE`.
- **Ready**: by the seller's people while the order is open; tells its customer to come.
- **Handing over**: by the seller's people, within the window, once they have matched the collector to the order by its QR code (the order's identifier) or by the name and phone number on it.
- **Refusing**: by the seller's people while the order is open, with `not_suitable`, `wrong_items`, `no_answer`, `no_show`, `late`, `sold_out` or `other` and a note of up to 200 characters. For `no_answer`, `no_show` and `late` the customer did not come, so the units go back to their offers; for the others they are not offered again.
- **Reviewing**: by the customer, signed in or with the access, once the order is `collected`; one review for each order, with 1 to 5 stars, any of the tags `fast_pickup`, `good_quality`, `good_variety` and `fair_portions` (each once; the web translates them) and an optional comment of up to 500 characters. A second review is refused. The review is read with its order, by everyone who reads the order, unless an admin hid it: then only admins read it, its customer is not told, and the order's `canReview` stays false (SEC-145). Hiding and showing a review are recorded in the audit as `review.hide` and `review.show`.
- **Reporting a problem** (SEC-84): by the customer, signed in or with the access, once the order is `collected` or `expired` and within two days of its collection or of its window's end; one report for each order, of the kind `missing_item`, `wrong_item`, `poor_quality` or `store_closed` (the web translates them), with an optional note of up to 500 characters and no control character but line breaks. The order's `canReportProblem` says whether its customer may still report one. The seller's people answer it once, in 1 to 500 characters. The report and its answer are read with the order by its customer, the seller's people and admins. A push tells the store the kind reported and the customer that the store answered, never the note or the answer.
- **Reopening**: by the seller's people, for an order that was handed over or refused and whose window is not over; it is confirmed again. The units a refusal had returned are taken again, and `ORDER_OFFER_SOLD_OUT` answers when they are gone. An order its customer cancelled stays cancelled.
- **Reading**: its customer, signed in or with the access, the seller's people and admins; anyone else gets 404. Admins read but never act for a seller.
- **Withdrawing an offer** ends the orders still to collect on it: `order` listens to `offer.withdrawn` and refuses each with the reason `withdrawn`, returning the units it held of the seller's other offers. Staff cannot choose that reason, and an order refused for it is not reopened ([SEC-108](../increments/completed/sec-108-withdraw-with-orders/design.md)).

- **The chat** (SEC-117): one chat for each order, created by its first message, between its customer and the seller's people; admins neither read nor write it. A message is 1 to 1,000 characters, trimmed, from a side (`customer` or `seller`, any of the seller's people writing as the seller), numbered from 1 by `order_chats.last_seq`, which serializes the messages of one chat in commit order. The same `Idempotency-Key` from the same side returns the message sent first, even after the chat has closed. A side sends at most 20 messages a minute (`ORDER_MESSAGES_TOO_FAST` with `Retry-After`), and a chat holds 500. Sending locks the order for share and refuses with `ORDER_CHAT_CLOSED` unless the order is `confirmed` or `ready` with its window not over, or `collected` less than 2 hours ago; a cancelled, refused or expired order closes its chat at once. Reading and marking seen work on a closed chat. Seen is kept for each side and only rises; any of the seller's people seeing counts as the seller. The order carries `unreadMessages` for its reader and `messageCount`, how many messages its chat holds, both absent for admins. Messages are deleted 30 days after the order's window ends (`order.forget_old_chats`, daily) and with the account that placed the order (`order.forget_account`). Logs hold the order, the side and `seq`, never the text.
- **Pushes** (SEC-25): every change a customer or a store must hear of writes a push in its transaction through `notification.QueuePush`, on the live socket's topics. A push names the dish and the store, the hours or the reason, never the customer's name or phone, an amount or a message's text.
  - **To the customer, on `order:{id}`, opening `/orders/{id}`:**
    - the order is ready;
    - the order was refused, with the reason, or withdrawn;
    - its hours moved;
    - a reminder;
    - the store wrote in the chat.
  - **The reminder.** `order.remind_due_soon`, every minute, marks each order still to collect whose window opens within 30 minutes (`due_soon_at`), once. It reminds only orders placed before those 30 minutes began.
  - **To the seller's people, on `seller:{sellerId}`, opening `/stores/{sellerId}/orders/{id}`:**
    - a new order;
    - the customer cancelled;
    - the customer wrote.
  - **No push** for a hand-over, an expiry, a reopening or a review.
- **Live tickets and notices** (SEC-117): the socket reads no cookie or `Order-Access`; it follows a topic only for a ticket, 256 random bits valid 30 seconds and used once (only its SHA-256 is stored in `order_live_tickets`; `order.purge_live_tickets` deletes the expired every 10 minutes). Every path that changes an order, and every message and seen mark, sends a notice in its transaction through `platform.Notify`, to `order:{id}` and `seller:{sellerId}`; a notice holds identifiers, a status and numbers, never a text or a name. The frames of `GET /live` are in the [platform behavior](platform.md#the-live-socket).

## Limits

- One client address places at most 60 orders in ten minutes, signed in or not; past it `PLATFORM_TOO_MANY_REQUESTS` (429) with `Retry-After` (SEC-160). A limit per person is SEC-158.

## Not yet

Per the [plan](../increments/completed/sec-15-order-module/plan.md): a limit of units per customer and day, and of orders per phone number, since a number is not verified; telling customers who have no push by Zalo, once a legal entity exists (SEC-25); paying online (SEC-18).
