# SEC-129 — The posting drawer's sources and "Món mới": design

Status: started on 21 October 2026. Tracked in Linear as SEC-129. Asked by the founder on seeing the drawer on a phone ([SEC-57](../../active/sec-57-seller-web/design.md), [SEC-55](../../completed/sec-55-seller-catalog/design.md)).

## Decision

- The dish step offers three sources, **Từ danh mục**, **Món đã đăng** and **Món mới**, in one row of three equal cells on every width, the words centred and smaller if they must be. They never wrap, and the top edge of a chip is never cut off by the scrolling body.
- "Món đột xuất" is **"Món mới"**: a dish typed and photographed in the drawer.

## Considered and not done

A dish posted from "Món mới" was to be added to the catalog as well. The founder withdrew it the same day: a new dish posted from the drawer is not saved in the catalog, as before.
