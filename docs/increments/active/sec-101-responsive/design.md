# SEC-101 — Responsive customer web: design

Status: started on 21 October 2026. Tracked in Linear as SEC-101. The owner asked for every customer page to work from a phone to a desktop. The work is in parts; this is part 1, the orders pages, after Figma frames W5 (155:6086) and W6 (185:13021), the order's status band at 1440, D6 (218:16389), "Đơn của tôi" at desktop, and WK7 (185:13407), the review card.

## Decisions

| #   | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Why                                                                                                                    |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Three layouts for the whole customer web: below 768px the phone's column; 768–1023px one wider column; from 1024px two columns inside a content width of 1168px (`lg:max-w-292`). Nothing scrolls sideways at 360px                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | The owner asked for every width to work; one rule for all pages keeps them alike                                       |
| 2   | From 768px the order's steps follow Figma W5 and W6 as one ordered list: "Đã đặt", "Chuẩn bị", "Sẵn sàng", "Đã nhận", the current one marked `aria-current="step"`, each a round icon beside its name and time, joined by 2px bars that stretch evenly. Below 768px the phone keeps its own row of icons ending "Hoàn tất" (Figma K6); only one of the two is shown                                                                                                        | The owner asked for the phone to stay as it is; one list shown, so a screen reader reads one                           |
| 3   | The times are the order's: `createdAt`, `readyAt`, `collectedAt`. Preparing has none, as the backend confirms an order as it is placed, so it shows only a line when it is the current step. The statuses map as SEC-97 decision 5 says; a collected order has its last step as the current one, as W6 draws it                                                                                                                                                            | No timestamp is invented                                                                                               |
| 4   | The longer line of the current step ("20:24 · mời bạn tới quầy") and the line of a step to come are shown from 768px only, in the wide steps                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | The phone keeps its own row, which has no times                                                                        |
| 5   | A step to come keeps `inverse-muted` for its words and takes the new `inverse-faint` (Figma tasco/300) for its dashed ring and bar only                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | tasco/300 on the blue is 4.1:1, too little for 12px text                                                               |
| 6   | From 1024px "Đơn của tôi" has two columns: the lists at the left, one card or row to a line, and at the right "Còn món ngon" with the two offers of tonight that close first, as offer cards. Below 1024px the right column is not shown. If the offers cannot be read the orders show without it                                                                                                                                                                          | Figma D6; the page leads back to ordering                                                                              |
| 7   | The review card's stars (32px) and chip checks are fixed in size and only the chips stretch; nothing in the code scales with the width, so WK7 needs no change                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Figma WK7 draws them scaled only because its vectors have the scale constraint                                         |
| 8   | The dish photograph's clock on the offer card stays as it was (14px); D6's 16px clock is not applied                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | The owner asked for the phone to stay as it is, and the card is shared                                                 |
| 9   | The phone (below 768px) and the desktop (768px and up) are two layouts, not one that stretches: the desktop starts at `md`, with the horizontal header and the 1168px column (`contentWidth` in `lib/layout.ts`) shared by the header, the footer, the top bars and the pages. Where the two differ in what they draw, each size has its own markup, shown by `md:hidden` and `hidden md:flex`                                                                             | The owner asked for it after seeing the search at 1680px; one component piled with breakpoints is harder to keep right |
| 10  | From 768px the customer web is the desktop layout (the owner's decision: phone below 768, desktop from it). The seller page (WK3) has the menu with a sticky index at the left and the cart's sidebar at the right from 1024px, under the menu from 768; the bottom bar is the phone's. The sidebar shows the cart as the checkout does: one card per seller with its dishes' − and +, "Tiền món", the total and "Đặt món · price" to the checkout                         | Figma WK3; one cart view, so the cart can be changed where it is read                                                  |
| 11  | The checkout (WK5) from 768px is `CheckoutDesktop`, chosen by `useIsMobile` since the cart is drawn on the client only: the cards, payment at the counter as a line and the policy at the left, the summary and "Đặt món" at the right from 1024px, under the cards below it. Who collects is asked in a dialog, not a sheet; a dish someone else took first (WK5b) is a centred dialog, "Đặt N đơn, bỏ món hết · price" removing the sold-out dishes and ordering at once | Figma WK5, WK5b                                                                                                        |
| 12  | The open order (D4) from 1024px is a grid of three columns: the ticket at the right of the blue band, "Cách nhận món" and "Huỷ đơn" under it in two, the details and the way to the counter in the third; below 1024px the cards stack. The collected and ended orders keep the two columns of decision 6's page                                                                                                                                                           | Figma D4                                                                                                               |
