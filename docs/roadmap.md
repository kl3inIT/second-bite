# Second Bite roadmap

Task status lives in Linear (team Second-bite, issues `SEC-<n>`); this page summarizes it and links to the increments.

## Delivered

- SEC-5 — Repository foundation, 16 October 2026: brief, vision, conventions, guidelines, the research done before design, the Go agent skills and the Linear MCP server.
- [SEC-10 — Backend foundation and the `seller` module](increments/completed/sec-10-backend-foundation/design.md), 17 October 2026: the Go service with its checks in CI, sign-in by emailed code with bearer sessions, email through the provider admins choose (SMTP or Brevo), and `seller` (opened by anyone, activated by admins, staffed by its owner). Specs and test matrices under `docs/specs/` and `docs/tests/`; Brevo's delivery reports moved to SEC-24.
- [SEC-52 — Legal, error and account screens](increments/completed/sec-52-legal-error-account-screens/design.md), 20 October 2026: redrawn in Figma as web desktop and mobile app; on the web under the seller header and findable by search and answer engines (metadata, language alternates, JSON-LD, sitemap); in the app the legal pages open inside it and every screen but the home has the blue top bar (PR #30). Staging to be measured again with PageSpeed once deployed.
- [SEC-37 — Account deletion and account settings](increments/completed/sec-37-account-deletion/design.md), 20 October 2026: a person deletes their account in the app or on the web with an emailed code, from the foot of account settings, which the account menu and the account tab lead to; `/delete-account` is also Google Play's web link. The first events between modules, through a River outbox (PR #33).
- [SEC-28 — Failure messages translated by the server](increments/completed/sec-28-failure-words/design.md), 20 October 2026: every failure in Vietnamese and English beside its code, chosen from `Accept-Language`; the web and the app show the server's words; proven against the real service (PR #38).
- [SEC-61 — Edit the display name](increments/completed/sec-61-display-name/design.md), 20 October 2026: a person names their account from account settings, on the web and in the app; a name they chose stays when they sign in with Google; a refused name reads in the server's words; proven against the real service (PR #39).
- [SEC-75 — Admin foundation](increments/completed/sec-75-admin-foundation/design.md), 21 October 2026: every `/admin` operation behind one group, refused signed out and to customers, with an append-only audit trail of admin changes; settings saved at the version read; admins granted and revoked from the web and the command line; a store's states `pending`, `active`, `rejected`, `blocked` with the owner emailed; the admin web's shell, stores, a store's page, settings, the log, accounts and admins (PRs #42, #57, #67).
- [SEC-66 — A store's real location, pinned on a map](increments/completed/sec-66-store-location-pin/design.md), 22 October 2026: the owner pins the store on a self-hosted map of Hanoi (MapLibre and a PMTiles extract served at `/map/`) when opening it, from a suggested address, the location button or the map, and moves it later; the server keeps every store in Hanoi, and a moved store moves its offers on sale and its orders still to collect; an admin sees the pin (PRs #107, #112, #113, #114).
- [SEC-15 — The `order` module](increments/completed/sec-15-order-module/design.md), 22 October 2026: a customer orders units of a store's dishes with a name and a phone number, signed in or not, and pays at the counter; the store marks the order ready, hands it over by its QR or by name and phone, refuses it with a reason or reopens it; the customer cancels until it is ready and rates it once collected; orders expire with their window and follow a withdrawn dish or a moved store. On the web only; built through SEC-76, SEC-86, SEC-78, SEC-121, SEC-117 and SEC-25 (PRs #45, #49, #51, #95). The offer page with its order panel (SEC-45) is part of it; the app's screens moved to SEC-12, transfers to SEC-18.
- [SEC-80 — The prototype's numbers](increments/completed/sec-80-prototype-numbers/design.md), 22 October 2026: the site counts the pages people open itself (no analytics service; bots, prefetches and driven browsers left out; a daily-salted visitor key, views kept 400 days); the admin's Báo cáo page shows views, orders held and collected, the share collected and what expired, per day and per store, and exports them as CSV; the poster's QR counts as `qr` (PRs #127, #132, #134, #137).
- [SEC-84 — A customer reports a problem with an order](increments/completed/sec-84-order-problem/design.md), 24 October 2026: within two days of a collected or expired order, its customer, signed in or holding its access, reports one problem (an item missing, the wrong item, poor quality, the store closed) with an optional note; the store answers it once on the order's page; each side is told by push without the words (PR #145).
- [SEC-82 — Feedback from a store to the team](increments/completed/sec-82-store-feedback/design.md), 24 October 2026: the customer's side is the review after a pick-up and SEC-84's report; a store's owner and staff send "Góp ý cho Second Bite" from the store's menu, kept without its author, at most 20 a day; admins read it on the report page, by the same days and store (PR #146).
- [SEC-162 — The store's staff on the web, and a store handed to its owner](increments/completed/sec-162-store-staff/design.md), 27 October 2026: the store menu's "Nhân viên" lets the owner add staff by email and remove them, and staff read the list and leave; admins hand a store the team opened to its owner from its members tab, the previous owner staying as staff, recorded as `seller.change_owner` (PR #166).
- [SEC-43 — Signing in on the app](increments/completed/sec-43-app-sign-in/design.md), 19 October 2026: the Flutter application's sign-in with the emailed code and Google, the session as a Bearer token in the platform's secure store, and the web's Lucide icons; to be checked on a device before the beta.
- [SEC-53 — The seller's web screens in Figma](increments/completed/sec-53-seller-web-design/design.md), 20 October 2026: sign-in and opening a store, the four tabs, posting, expected surplus, managing and editing posts, the catalog; built on the web through SEC-57, SEC-78, SEC-79 and SEC-102.
- The seller's web, through 22 October 2026: posting and its drawer ([SEC-129](increments/completed/sec-129-drawer-new-dish/design.md), [SEC-119](increments/completed/sec-119-three-photos/design.md)), orders by step with an order's own page ([SEC-102](increments/completed/sec-102-seller-order-steps/design.md)), handing over without a code ([SEC-86](increments/completed/sec-86-collect-without-code/design.md)), every store screen on a phone ([SEC-79](increments/completed/sec-79-seller-web-phone/design.md)), the store's home ([SEC-124](increments/completed/sec-124-store-home-dashboard/design.md)) and editing a store ([SEC-144](increments/completed/sec-144-edit-store/design.md)); SEC-46, the issue that asked for them, is closed with them.
- [SEC-33 — Staging](increments/completed/sec-33-staging/design.md), 18 October 2026: `secondbite.vadan.app` on the shared host as its own Compose project, deployed after every green `main`, with a database dump before each migration and nightly backups; from then on migrations are forward-only.
- SEC-57's seller rules, 21 October 2026: [a post's hours may change with orders, and the orders follow](increments/completed/sec-91-change-hours-with-orders/design.md) (SEC-91, PR #56); [ended posts and orders closed by a scheduled job](increments/completed/sec-96-close-ended/design.md) (SEC-96, PR #63); [withdrawing a post ends its orders](increments/completed/sec-108-withdraw-with-orders/design.md) (SEC-108, PR #70); [the address filled from where the person stands](increments/completed/sec-110-address-from-location/design.md) (SEC-110, PR #74); [the Đơn tab scans the customer's order QR](increments/completed/sec-113-scan-order-qr/design.md) (SEC-113, PR #79).
- [SEC-6 — Pilot domain model](increments/completed/sec-6-pilot-domain-model/design.md), 16 October 2026: the pilot's journey worked through boundary discovery, ending in the modules built since, their data owners and working assumptions; the reading of comparable products was SEC-8.
- [SEC-29 — Signing in on the web](increments/completed/sec-29-web-sign-in/design.md), with SEC-27, 19 October 2026: the session in an httpOnly cookie, the sign-in page with the emailed code and Google, linking by Google's authoritative addresses.
- [SEC-11 — The `offer` module](increments/completed/sec-11-offer-module/design.md), 18 October 2026: sellers post surplus with a photo and post it again; customers find offers near them; photos in the `file` module. Expected surplus moved to SEC-54; weekly schedules and followers' emails to SEC-190.
- [SEC-57 — The seller's web screens](increments/completed/sec-57-seller-web/design.md), 20 October 2026: open a store, my stores, the store's tabs, posting, editing and withdrawing (PRs #31, #32); the order tab came with [SEC-78](increments/completed/sec-78-pilot-holds/design.md) (21 October), the store's lists of reviews with [SEC-154](increments/completed/sec-154-seller-reviews/design.md) (26 October), and posting's prices and hours with [two phone reviews](increments/completed/posting-price-time-layout/design.md) ([wheels](increments/completed/pickup-time-wheels/design.md), 23 October).
- [SEC-62 — The admin's stores](increments/completed/sec-62-admin-stores/design.md), 22 October 2026: the list, a store's page with its posts, members and orders; admins [delete a store softly and restore it](increments/completed/seller-soft-delete/design.md) since 31 October (SEC-187, PR #192).
- [Customer place](increments/completed/customer-place/design.md), 31 October 2026: the home shows every shop until a customer chooses a place, by location, a typed place or Tasco Mall, remembers it, and names "Gần tôi" by its street (PRs #185, #188, #189).
- SEC-185 — Production, 31 October 2026: `secondbite.vadan.app` is production, deployed by hand after an approval, indexed by search engines; staging moved to `preview.secondbite.vadan.app`, with the app's web preview at `/mobile/` ([runbook](runbooks/ci-cd.md)).
- [SEC-192 — The report's three numbers](increments/completed/sec-192-report-three-numbers/design.md), 31 October 2026: the admin opens on yesterday's report, led by new users, orders and visitors, each against the period before; "Hôm qua", "7 ngày" and "30 ngày" in one tap; "Sao chép báo cáo" copies the three numbers as a message; the CSV gains new accounts and first-time customers (PR #197).
- [SEC-25 — Web Push for orders](increments/completed/sec-25-web-push/design.md), 31 October 2026: customers and sellers are told of an order's events by Web Push, on production since 22 October 2026 and proved on real phones on 31 October; the offer on the order page made quieter in SEC-189.
- [SEC-193 — Sign in and register with a username and a password](increments/completed/sec-193-username-sign-in/design.md), 4 November 2026: a third way in beside the emailed code and Google, which the web's sign-in screen opens on; an account made so has no email, is named by its username and deleted with its password (PRs #200, #204). The app's screens follow in their own issue.

- The customer's web, through October 2026: [SEC-49 — looking around](increments/completed/sec-49-web-flow-1/design.md), [SEC-76 — the flow end to end](increments/completed/sec-76-web-customer-flow/design.md), [SEC-88 — the seller's page holds the dishes](increments/completed/sec-88-web-seller-page-dish-sheet/design.md), [SEC-83 — the orders list and an order's page](increments/completed/sec-83-web-orders-figma/design.md), [SEC-97 — pickup and review](increments/completed/sec-97-pickup-and-review/design.md), [SEC-109 — favourites](increments/completed/sec-109-favourites/design.md), [SEC-117 — chat on each order and live status](increments/completed/sec-117-order-chat-live/design.md), [SEC-175 — more kinds of dish](increments/completed/sec-175-dish-kinds/design.md) and [SEC-101 — responsive pages](increments/completed/sec-101-responsive/design.md), whose seller page, cart and audit at three widths were left undone.
- Stores and admins, through October 2026: [SEC-55 — the seller's catalog](increments/completed/sec-55-seller-catalog/design.md), [SEC-65 — an owner-less seller cannot be approved](increments/completed/sec-65-ownerless-approval/design.md), [SEC-126 — account deletion's follow-ups](increments/completed/sec-126-deletion-follow-ups/design.md), and every post, order and review for admins ([SEC-134](increments/completed/sec-134-admin-posts/design.md), [SEC-135](increments/completed/sec-135-admin-orders/design.md), [SEC-145](increments/completed/sec-145-admin-reviews/design.md)).
