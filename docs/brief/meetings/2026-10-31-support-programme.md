# The two-week support programme — team chat, 31 October 2026

Kept as received, from the messages and a screenshot of the chat: a thread of the team's group chat on 31 October 2026, in which Tùng proposed a support programme for stores and customers and the sponsor (Laura) answered, following the [meeting of 30 October](2026-10-30-trials-meeting.md). Only the layout changed: the sponsor's reply quoted Tùng's message in full, and the quote is left out here. It holds no contact details.

## What was decided

The team's reading, in English; the messages below are the record.

- The programme starts on Monday 2 November 2026 with La Paris, and Tùng sends the sponsor the number of orders each day.
- **Stores:** 5 stores in week 1, 10 to 15 in week 2. Each store's rewards over the two weeks, as the sponsor set them at 10:18:
  - posting on at least 10 of the 14 days, 7 to 10 portions a day: 200,000 VND;
  - selling about 50 portions over the two weeks (about 5 a day): 200,000 VND more;
  - the store that sells the most portions each week: 300,000 VND;
  - introducing Second Bite on the store's Facebook or Zalo page: 50,000 VND.
- **Customers:** 10,000 VND off a new customer's first order and 5,000 VND off each later one, collected in person; about 2.5 million VND for customers.
- **Budget:** about 6 million VND, raised from Tùng's 2 million.
- **The team's targets:** week 1, 5 stores posting steadily, about 25 orders a day, about 150 orders and 100 new customers; week 2, 10 to 15 stores, about 50 orders a day by its end, 400 to 500 orders over the two weeks and at least 30% of customers buying a second time. The sponsor's reason for more stores: with Tùng's targets each store would sell one or two orders a day, too few to keep posting.
