# The two-week support programme — team chat, 31 October 2026

Kept as received: a thread of the team's group chat on 31 October 2026, in which Tùng proposed a support programme for stores and customers and the sponsor (Laura) answered, following the [meeting of 30 October](2026-10-30-trials-meeting.md). Only the layout changed: the sponsor's reply quoted Tùng's message in full, and the quote is left out here. It holds no contact details.

## What was decided

The team's reading, in English; the messages below are the record.

- The programme starts on Monday 2 November 2026 with La Paris, and Tùng sends the sponsor the number of orders each day.
- **Stores:** 5 stores in week 1, 10 to 15 in week 2, each posting about 5 portions a day. A store that posts on at least 10 of the 14 days, with real food of good quality, and gives feedback afterwards receives 200,000 VND. The store that sells the most portions each week receives 300,000 VND; a store that introduces Second Bite on its Facebook or Zalo page receives 50,000 VND.
- **Customers:** 10,000 VND off a new customer's first order and 5,000 VND off each later one, collected in person; about 2.5 million VND for customers.
- **Budget:** about 6 million VND, raised from Tùng's 2 million.
- **Targets:** about 50 orders a day by the end of week 2, 400 to 500 orders over the two weeks, and at least 30% of customers buying a second time. The sponsor's reason: with Tùng's targets each store would sell one or two orders a day, too few to keep posting; more orders need more stores.
