# Second Bite architecture

This page states what is implemented today. Intended outcomes are in [docs/vision.md](docs/vision.md); planned work is in [docs/roadmap.md](docs/roadmap.md) and never appears here as a fact. Update this page in the change that makes a statement true or false.

## Runtime overview

The backend and the web application run on a developer's machine, in tests, and as container images (`server/Dockerfile`, `web/Dockerfile`) that a release deploys to staging with Docker Compose on a shared host ([releases and delivery](docs/runbooks/ci-cd.md)). The Flutter application in `mobile/` shows the customer home and the account tab, reading offers through a client generated from `api/openapi.yaml`, signs in with the emailed code or Google and keeps the session's Bearer token in the platform's secure store (Keychain, Keystore), shows the terms and the privacy policy inside the app at `/terms` and `/privacy`, answers an unknown address with a not-found screen, and is built in CI.

- One Go binary, `secondbite` (`server/cmd/secondbite`). At startup it reads its configuration, opens a PostgreSQL pool, applies the pending migrations under a lock, and serves the HTTP API until SIGINT or SIGTERM, then lets requests in flight finish ([runbook](docs/runbooks/backend.md)).
- PostgreSQL 18 with PostGIS 3.6, with the tables of `identity`, `notification`, `seller`, `file`, `offer`, `order` and `audit`; files are kept in PostgreSQL unless an S3-compatible store is configured.
- People sign in with a six-digit code emailed to them and get a session token, sent as `Authorization: Bearer`. Email goes through the provider admins chose (SMTP or Brevo's API); Mailpit catches it in development.
- People open sellers, active at once; an admin approves, rejects with a reason or blocks with a reason, and the owner is emailed. An owner adds staff by email. Who may publish for a seller is answered by `seller`, for the offers to come.
- Customers order units of one seller's offers with a name and a phone number, signed in or not, confirmed at once and collected at the counter within one window, staff matching the collector by the order's QR code or its name and phone number; the seller's people mark orders ready, hand them over and refuse them; the customer rates a collected order once, with stars, tags and a comment; the customer pays the seller at the counter.
- Signed-in people upload photos, which are written again as JPEG in two sizes without their metadata and served to anyone at `/photos/{id}/1600.jpg` and `/400.jpg`; a seller's owner sets one as the seller's photo.
- A signed-in person deletes their account with a code emailed to its address: the account, its sessions, its Google link and the emails to its address go at once, and through the outbox `seller` removes its memberships and favourites (blocking a seller it owned) and `file` its unused photos ([identity](docs/specs/identity.md)).
- Modules tell each other what happened through a transactional outbox on River, in the same PostgreSQL ([platform](docs/specs/platform.md#events-between-modules)); `serve` runs the listeners.
- Two administrative commands set up a fresh installation: `secondbite email configure` and `secondbite admin grant`; `secondbite admin revoke` takes an admin away, never the last. Admins also grant and revoke admins through the API, and every admin change is recorded in the audit trail.
- The API answers the probes `/livez` and `/readyz`, serves its OpenAPI 3.1 contract, and answers every error, an unknown route included, as an RFC 9457 problem with a `code` and the request's `requestId`.
- Every request has an identifier, returned in `X-Request-Id` and logged as `request_id`.
- The web application (`web/`, Next.js) has the customer's screens of the product, reading `GET /offers` and `GET /offers/{id}` through the generated SDK. The customer home shows tonight's offers at Tasco Mall, or around the person's place once the browser shares it (`?near=`), with filters by kind; the cards show what can be ordered, then what has sold out. The search (`/search`) finds dishes by words, or groups them by seller, narrowed by distance, highest price, collection time and kind, in a chosen order; the recent searches stay in the browser only (`localStorage`). A dish opens on its seller's page, scrolled to its tinted row (`/sellers/{id}?at=`), and its photograph or name opens its sheet with its description, weight and notes and how many to add; `/offers/{id}` leads there; a seller (`/sellers/{id}`) shows their dishes tonight, searchable by name, built from `GET /offers?sellerId=` since the seller's own record is for their people. A cart of dishes from any sellers, never more than the portions left, is kept in the browser (`localStorage`) and confirmed at `/cart`, which asks who collects (a name and a phone number, remembered in the browser), checks the number once in a dialog, then places one order per seller (`POST /orders`) under one checkout, signed in or not, and opens them as one order, one part per seller; when a dish ran out first it says which and offers to order what is left. An order placed without signing in keeps its access in an httpOnly cookie, `secondbite_orders`. The customer follows an order at `/orders/[orderId]` with its steps, its QR code once ready (the order's identifier, drawn on the server with `uqr`) beside who collects it, and a free cancel until the window opens, or its receipt once ended; and lists their orders at `/orders`, the bottom bar's "Đơn của tôi", those still to collect as cards and the ended ones under their day in the app's time zone, those of one checkout as one: the account's, or those this browser placed; with neither, it asks them to sign in. Besides these, the terms of use and the privacy policy (`/terms`, `/privacy`, drafts until the founder and a lawyer review them, linked from every page's foot), a language switch and not-found and error pages, in Vietnamese under `/vi` and English under `/en`. Public pages name their canonical address and their other language, describe themselves in JSON-LD and are listed in `/sitemap.xml`; private pages carry `noindex`, and `/robots.txt` keeps every crawler out until a production address exists. Under `/admin`, an admin has a sidebar frame with the stores (`/admin/sellers`: a card per status with its last 14 days, counted by `seller` since 21 October 2026, that filters the list, words, a range of days of the last change, a sort and the page size in the address, and from a row's menu block with a reason, unblock, approve or reject; a store's page with its posts by status and its members), the log of what admins did (`/admin/audit`) and the email settings; anyone else gets the site's not-found page. It calls the API as `/api/...` on its own origin through a client generated from `api/openapi.yaml`; in development Next.js forwards those calls to the Go server ([web guideline](docs/guidelines/web.md)).

## Code and capability boundaries

The modules under `server/internal/`, found through the [pilot domain model](docs/increments/active/sec-6-pilot-domain-model/design.md); each root package is the module's published API.

| Module                                       | Owns                                                                                                                                                           | Published API                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [`identity`](docs/specs/identity.md)         | Accounts and their role (customer or admin), emailed sign-in codes, sessions (tables `identity_*`)                                                             | `Service`: request a code, sign in, sign out, read an account, list, grant and revoke admins (never oneself, never the last), delete the account with its own code; `Authenticate`, the middleware that puts the caller in each request's context; `AccountDeletedEvent`                                                                                      |
| [`seller`](docs/specs/seller.md)             | Sellers, their status set by admins (pending, active, rejected, blocked) with its reason, their owner and staff, and the customers who follow them (tables `seller_*`)                                                  | `Service`: open, read and change a seller, add and remove staff, list, approve, reject and block for admins; `Publishing`, which checks the right to publish and returns the seller's name and collection point, `Publics` and `Blocked`, for offers; `IsActiveIn`, read in an order's transaction; follow, unfollow and list a customer's followed sellers |
| [`offer`](docs/specs/offer.md)               | The surplus sellers post, and the settings every offer follows (tables `offer_*`)                                                                              | `Service`: post, change, withdraw and read offers, search them near a point by Vietnamese words and filters, a seller's list, the settings for admins                                                                                           |
| [`order`](docs/specs/order.md)               | Orders for units of one seller's offers, their lines and every transition (tables `order_*`)                                                                   | `Service`: place, read and list a customer's orders, cancel, rate; a seller's orders of a day, ready, hand over against the code, refuse                                                                                                              |
| [`file`](docs/specs/file.md)                 | The files the system keeps, in PostgreSQL or an S3-compatible store as configuration chooses, and the photos uploaded for offers and sellers (tables `file_*`) | `Service`: upload a photo, serve it, `Claim` it for an offer or a seller                                                                                                                                                                        |
| [`audit`](docs/specs/audit.md)               | What admins did, in a catalog of actions, kept with the change it records (table `audit_events`)                                                               | `Service`: record an action in the change's transaction, forget a deleted account's address                                                                                                                                                    |
| [`notification`](docs/specs/notification.md) | Who delivers email, chosen by admins while the service runs, and the record of emails sent (tables `notification_*`)                                           | `Service`: send an email, read and save the email settings                                                                                                                                                                                      |

`internal/platform` is not a module: it holds the technical code every module uses ([behavior](docs/specs/platform.md)), including the caller of a request (`platform.CallerFrom`, `RequireCaller`, `RequireAdmin`), so a module authorizes without depending on `identity`.

### Allowed module dependencies

One line per module, naming the modules whose published API it may use, such as "- `seller` → `identity`". A module not listed may use none. `internal/platform` depends on no module, and every module may use it. The boundary test (`server/internal/boundaries_test.go`) reads this list.

- `file` → `identity`
- `identity` → `audit`, `notification`
- `notification` → `audit`
- `offer` → `audit`, `file`, `identity`, `seller`
- `order` → `audit`, `file`, `identity`, `offer`, `seller`
- `seller` → `audit`, `file`, `identity`, `notification`
