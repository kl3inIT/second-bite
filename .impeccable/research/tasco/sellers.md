# F&B sellers in and around Tasco Mall Long Biên

Desk research, 17 October 2026. Nobody contacted any seller. These are web sources only, and every row needs an on-site check before it goes into a pilot plan or a design.

## The site

- **Name and address.** TASCO Mall, Số 7-9 đường Nguyễn Văn Linh, phường Việt Hưng, Hà Nội. The mall's own website uses this post-2025 ward name. Older listings give the old wards Gia Thụy, Phúc Đồng or Bồ Đề in quận Long Biên. One blog prints "79 Nguyễn Văn Linh", which is a typo. Sources: [tascomall.com tenant page](https://www.tascomall.com/b/la-paris), [Phúc Long opening post](https://www.tascomall.com/p/khai-truong-phuc-long-tai-tasco-mall-vo-van-uu-dai).
- **Former name.** The building used to be **Savico Megamall**, also called the Trung tâm thương mại Gia Thụy, khu X2. Many Foody and ShopeeFood listings still use that name, or "BigC Savico".
- **Hours.** The mall is open 09:00-22:00 every day ([Visit page](https://www.tascomall.com/en/ghe-tham), [Apple Maps](https://maps.apple.com/place?place-id=ICB9ACE6A62E56CB7)).
- **Layout.** Three trading floors and a basement. Tenants are on Tầng hầm (B1), Tầng 1, Tầng 2 and Tầng 3.
- **Supermarket.** The basement holds a GO! hypermarket (Central Retail). It was Big C Long Biên and became GO! Long Biên in July 2024. The mall's online directory does not list it.
- **Food court.** The mall's directory shows no food court. Third-party pages describe a "khu ẩm thực tự chọn" with several stalls inside or beside GO! in the basement. Its current form is **[unverified]**.
- **Weekend night market.** "Phố đêm TASCO Mall – Say Hi Long Biên" was relaunched on 18/10/2024 with **30+ stalls** of food, games and handmade goods. It runs Friday evening and all day Saturday and Sunday in the event yard ([source](https://www.tascomall.com/p/pho-dem-tasco-mall-say-hi-long-bien)). Nothing found confirms that it still runs in 2026: **[unverified]**.
- **What the mall's directory lists today** ([Quán ăn & nhà hàng](https://www.tascomall.com/quan-an), [Cafe & giải khát](https://www.tascomall.com/giai-khat)):
  - 16 restaurant brands.
  - 8 café and drink brands.
  - It gives floors only: no unit numbers and no hours.
- **Older names on the English page.** [The English directory](https://tascomall.com/en/quan-an) still shows Sumo Yakiniku and Quán Ngon Miền Tây, which the current Vietnamese directory no longer lists. Floors differ between pages, so the Vietnamese directory is taken as current. The Decathlon blog floors conflict with both and are ignored.

## Per-seller notes

### Most promising for genuine surplus

**1. La Paris (T1).**
- **Brand:** a Hà Nội French pâtisserie with 6 shops: Tây Hồ, Hoàn Kiếm, Vincom Bà Triệu, Times City, Thăng Long and Tasco Mall Long Biên ([laparis.com.vn](https://www.laparis.com.vn/vi), [mall page](https://www.tascomall.com/b/la-paris)).
- **Prices:** individual entremets (Capucine, Apolline, Léonie, Solène) at 125,000. A box of 6 cupcakes at 240,000. Birthday cakes from 430,000.
- **Surplus:** croissants and viennoiserie baked that morning, plus entremets that will not keep another day.
- **Packing:** paper bag for bread; a clear plastic dome or a kraft or white cake box for entremets.
- **Recruitment:** a small owner-run chain, so the owner can probably decide alone. Prices are high for the target customers: a 50% markdown still leaves ~60,000 per entremet.
- **Open questions:** hours, and whether it bakes on site or ships in from a central kitchen.

**2. Paris by La Parisienne – Tasco Mall.**
- **Listing:** Foody gives "Trung tâm Thương mại BigC Savico Long Biên", open 07:30-22:00, with birthday and cream cakes, takeaway and delivery ([Foody](https://www.foody.vn/ha-noi/paris-by-la-parisienne-tasco-mall-long-bien)).
- **Same unit as La Paris?** Possibly. The mall directory lists only "La Paris", so this may be the same unit under an older name: **[unverified]**.
- **Branches:** there is a second branch at 301 Nguyễn Văn Cừ.
- **Surplus:** sweet buns, sliced bread and cake slices.
- **Packing:** paper or plastic bags, and clear clamshells for slices.

**3. GO! Long Biên (B1).**
- **Store:** Central Retail hypermarket. Big C became GO! on 01/07/2024. Open 08:00-22:00 ([thongtindiadiem, updated 7/9/2025](https://thongtindiadiem.com/go-long-bien-co-gi-kham-pha-diem-mua-sam-an-uong-giai-tri-hap-dan/), [GuideBold](https://guidebold.com/guide/big-c-long-bien-savico-megamall-ha-noi/), [GO! Long Biên Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/GOLongBienvn/)).
- **Cheap lunches:** a TikTok surfaced by search (5/10/2026) describes lunches under 50,000 at GO Long Biên: **[unverified]**.
- **Surplus:** GO!/Big C stores typically run a bakery (bánh mì, bánh bao, sweet buns), a hot deli (gà rán, xôi, cơm phần, chả, nem) and cut fruit. This is the largest surplus volume in the building.
- **Packing:** foam or PP plastic trays with cling film, or clear clamshells for the deli; plastic bags for bread.
- **Existing discounts:** only campaign discounts were found. Midnight Sale 2024 ran from 50% off at 17-22h, and "Chợ sớm giảm sung" gives 10% off fresh food ([congthuong](https://congthuong.vn/ha-noi-dem-khong-khong-ngu-co-hoi-mua-thuc-pham-giam-gia-toi-70-tai-big-c-148218.html)). No evidence of a published daily end-of-day markdown, though in-store yellow-sticker reductions are common in practice: **[unverified]**.
- **Who decides:** markdown policy is set by Central Retail head office, so the local store manager cannot sign up alone.

**4. Bami Việt (34 Nguyễn Văn Linh, outside).**
- **Shop:** an independent bánh mì shop, open 06:00-22:00.
- **Prices:** no Long Biên menu found; the Bami Bread brand elsewhere sells at 15,000-30,000 **[unverified]**.
- **Surplus:** baguettes baked that day and cooked fillings.
- **Packing:** paper bag or paper wrap.
- **Fit:** the easiest independent to recruit, and the price fits the customer profile.

**5. Highlands Coffee (T1).**
- **Shop:** 07:00-22:00 ([menuqr](https://menuqr.vn/ha-noi/highlands-coffee-tra-ca-phe-banh-savico-long-bien/)). Its menu at this shop shows Bánh mì gà xé, Bánh Tiramisu and Bánh Mousse Cacao. Prices could not be fetched from the official site (403): **[unverified]**.
- **Surplus:** the bánh mì and cake slices are delivered daily from a central kitchen and have a short shelf life.
- **Packing:** paper sleeve for bánh mì; plastic clamshell for slices.
- **Who decides:** Highlands (JFC) sets policy centrally, with 1,011 outlets at March 2026.

**6. Starbucks (T1, stall 105A).**
- **Shop:** 07:00-21:30 on ShopeeFood, 08:00-22:00 on Foody, 80,000-150,000 per person ([Foody](https://www.foody.vn/ha-noi/starbucks-coffee-tasco-mall-long-bien), [ShopeeFood](https://shopeefood.vn/ha-noi/starbucks-coffee-tasco-mall-long-bien)).
- **Surplus:** pastries and sandwiches with a same-day date.
- **Packing:** paper bags.
- **Who decides:** the franchise is set centrally (Tata Starbucks in Vietnam). Too expensive for the target customers even at 50% off.

**7. Phúc Long (T1, S101-102).**
- **Shop:** opened on 24/04 (year not stated, likely 2026) with "bánh tặng kèm" (a free pastry) among the opening offers. Open 07:00-21:30, delivery and takeaway ([Foody](https://www.foody.vn/ha-noi/phuc-long-tasco-mall), [opening post](https://www.tascomall.com/p/khai-truong-phuc-long-tai-tasco-mall-vo-van-uu-dai)).
- **Surplus:** a small pastry and cookie case.
- **Who decides:** Masan, centrally.

### Restaurants with limited, cooked-to-order surplus

**8. Nét Huế (T3).**
- **Shop:** Huế food: bún bò Huế, cơm hến, nem lụi. 150,000-300,000 per person per a blog ([mall page](https://www.tascomall.com/b/net-hue), [Decathlon blog](https://www.decathlon.vn/blog/tasco-mall-co-gi-an/)).
- **Surplus:** broth and steamed cakes (bánh bèo, nậm, lọc) made ahead.
- **Packing:** plastic soup tubs, foil or banana-leaf-wrapped bánh nậm/lọc.
- **Fit:** a small chain, so it may decide locally.

**9. Panda Chef (T3).** "Robot chef", 100+ Asian dishes cooked fresh ([mall page](https://www.tascomall.com/b/panda-chef)). Surplus is mostly prepped raw ingredients, which are not sellable to the public. Low fit.

**10. Joopii (T3).** A Korean tteokbokki brand ([mall page](https://www.tascomall.com/b/joopii)). Possible surplus: kimbap and rice balls made ahead, packed in plastic boxes. Its model (buffet or à la carte) is **[unverified]**.

**11. Chả cá Lăng Đông Kinh (T2).** An independent chả cá restaurant ([mall page](https://www.tascomall.com/b/cha-ca-lang-dong-kinh)). The fish is cooked at the table, so little ready surplus. A possible "raw marinated fish kit" offer would raise food-safety questions.

**12-13. Lẩu Cá Kèo Sáu Ngân (T3), Lẩu Cá Kèo Miền Tây (B1).** Hotpot restaurants. Surplus would be raw ingredients, which do not fit the pickup model.

### Chains whose surplus policy is set centrally and are hard to recruit

**14-21. KFC (B1), Lotteria (B1), Popeyes (T3, stall 304), Domino's (T3), Pizza Hut (T3), Gogi House (T3), Kichi-Kichi (T3), Dookki (T3).**
- **Hours and prices:**
  - KFC: 100,000-200,000 per person (Decathlon blog).
  - Lotteria: from 40,000, 08:00-22:00 ([list.vn](https://list.vn/quan-an-ngon-tai-tasco-mall-long-bien-ha-noi-17391.htm)).
  - Popeyes: 10:00-22:00 ([Foody](https://www.foody.vn/ha-noi/ga-ran-popeyes-tasco-mall-ha-noi)).
  - Gogi House: from 200,000.
  - Kichi-Kichi: from 269,000.
  - Dookki: from 139,000, 10:30-22:00.
- **Surplus:** fast food (fried chicken held in the warmer) is discarded under brand hold-time rules.
- **Who decides:** buffets have no takeaway surplus. Golden Gate (Gogi, Kichi-Kichi), Yum/KFC, Lotte, Jollibee/RBI and Domino's all set food-waste policy at head office.

**22. Poseidon (T2).** A seafood buffet at 300,000-500,000 per person. Not a fit.

### Drinks brands (low surplus)

**23-29. Dingtea, Cha Panda, Mixue, Chago, Cha Tra Thai, Bingxue (B1, BK5), LaBoong (B1, ~40,000 per drink), ToCoToCo (B1, BK6, 09:30-22:00).**
- **Surplus:** drinks are made to order. Brewed tea and tapioca pearls are thrown out each night but cannot be sold as items.
- **Sources:** [Bingxue SF](https://shopeefood.vn/ha-noi/tra-sua-bingxue-go-long-bien), [LaBoong SF](https://shopeefood.vn/ha-noi/laboong-tra-o-long-lang-la-boong-go-long-bien), [ToCoToCo Foody](https://www.foody.vn/ha-noi/tra-sua-tocotoco-go-long-bien), [Savico area on Foody](https://www.foody.vn/ha-noi/khu-vuc-savico-megamall).

### Night market (event yard, weekends)

**Phố đêm TASCO Mall.** 30+ stalls of street food and snacks: xiên nướng, bánh tráng, chè, and similar. Prices are typically 15,000-50,000 **[unverified]**. Packing is paper cups, paper boats and plastic boxes. These are the most independent, price-appropriate sellers in the area. Their surplus at 22:00 on Sunday is real, but the stalls do not trade during the week. Whether the market is still running is **[unverified]**.
